Our story

A mother’s passion, stitched into a dream.

Janadhish started with something our mother has loved doing for over 25 years: stitching and creating clothes. Kiran Lodha has always enjoyed making outfits, trying new designs, and turning simple pieces of fabric into something beautiful. What began as a passion slowly grew into a dream of having something of her own.

That’s how Janadhish came into being.

We started this brand with one simple thought: clothes should be comfortable, beautiful, and made the way you like them. We love Indian fabrics, cottons, handblock prints, and designs you can wear and feel good in.

At Janadhish, you don’t always have to settle for what’s already available. Choose your fabric, colour, and size, and customise your outfit to match what you have in mind.

For us, this brand is very personal. It’s about our mother’s years of experience, her love for stitching, and our little dream of turning that passion into something bigger.

Welcome to Janadhish ♥️ We’re happy you’re here!
