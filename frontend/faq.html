Frequently asked questions

Helpful answers for shoppers

How do I place a custom order?

Choose a garment type, fabric, design, and fill in detailed measurements on the custom-made page.

Do you offer shipping?

Shipping is available and calculated at checkout. Pricing will update based on order value and destination.

Can I change my size after ordering?

Yes, for ready-to-wear pieces, changes may be possible before dispatch. Custom orders should be reviewed before final confirmation.
