## Shipping and delivery

JANADHISH currently accepts orders for delivery within India. Standard shipping
costs ₹250 when the merchandise subtotal is below ₹3,000 and is complimentary
when the subtotal is ₹3,000 or more. Any express delivery request is subject to
our confirmation; an express charge is not collected at checkout.

We begin order processing after the UPI payment has been verified against the
receiving account. We will share an estimated dispatch date of [DISPATCH WINDOW]
and a delivery estimate of [DELIVERY WINDOW] for the destination. Custom pieces
may take longer; we will confirm their timing before accepting a custom order.
If a shipment is delayed, contact us using [SUPPORT EMAIL] or [SUPPORT PHONE]
with your order number. Confirm the carrier, serviceable pin codes, and handling

## Refunds and cancelled orders

An order placed through the QR remains **awaiting payment** until the customer
submits a UPI transaction reference or requests cancellation. A cancellation
request holds the order and reserved stock until our team checks the receiving
account. A submitted reference remains **under review** until our team matches
the receiving account, reference, and exact amount. A submitted
reference or screenshot does not itself prove payment.

If a payment is missing, duplicated, sent for the wrong amount, or sent after an
order was cancelled, contact [SUPPORT EMAIL] with the order number and reference.
We will investigate against the receiving account before deciding how to resolve
it. For an accepted cancellation or return, we will initiate a refund to the
verified payer through [REFUND METHOD] within [REFUND INITIATION WINDOW] after
approval. The receiving bank or UPI provider may take additional time to post
the credit. Never request a customer's UPI PIN, password, or OTP.

Confirm who can approve refunds, how approval is recorded, and the exact return
and refund timeframes before publication.

## Privacy notice

JANADHISH collects information customers enter to use the store: name, email,
phone, delivery address, account credentials, garment measurements, custom
request details, contact messages, and the UPI reference and payer name submitted
for an order. We use this information to operate accounts, fulfil orders, review
payments, respond to requests, and handle disputes. Passwords are stored as
hashes. We do not collect a UPI PIN or payment card number in checkout.

The site stores the shopping bag and wishlist in the browser. It uses a session
token for signed-in access. The stylist uses the local catalog by default. If the shopper opts in, it may send a question and limited
product catalog context to [AI PROVIDER, IF ENABLED]; explain this clearly in
the assistant before launch. Hosting and other service providers may process
data to run the site. The page also loads fonts from Google and links to
Instagram; review whether those external requests match the approved privacy
approach.

Contact [PRIVACY CONTACT] to request access, correction, deletion, or help with
your data. We retain order and payment review records for [RETENTION PERIOD]
subject to applicable legal duties. List each processor, location, retention
period, and contact route before publishing this notice.

## Store terms

Product prices are shown in INR. The final amount, including standard shipping,
is calculated by our server when the customer starts checkout. An order created
at checkout is a request to purchase; we confirm fulfilment only after payment
is verified and stock is available. Customers must pay the exact displayed amount
to the JANADHISH UPI QR recipient and submit the actual transaction reference.
Submitting a false or reused reference does not establish payment.

Product images show the design as closely as practical; colour and texture can
vary by screen and material. Garment measurements and custom requests are
reviewed separately and do not themselves place a paid custom order. Customers
may request cancellation of an unpaid order through its private checkout link.
The order stays open until the receiving account is checked and cancellation is
confirmed. A paid order or a submitted payment reference requires support review.

Add the legal seller name, business address, support contact, grievance contact,
tax details if applicable, governing process, and approved cancellation and
returns terms before publishing.
