## Shipping, returns and refunds — proposed public copy

**Where we deliver.** JANADHISH currently accepts online orders for delivery within India. Availability at a particular PIN code is subject to courier serviceability. If we cannot fulfil an order, we will contact you using the details supplied at checkout and arrange the appropriate resolution.

**Shipping charges.** Standard shipping is ₹250 when the product subtotal is below ₹3,000 and complimentary when it is ₹3,000 or more. The checkout total shows the amount to pay for the selected items and standard shipping. Selecting “Express request” asks our team to check availability; it does not itself guarantee faster delivery or add a charge. We will discuss any additional express charge before you agree to it.

**Dispatch and delivery.** Ready-made orders are normally prepared within **[confirmed dispatch period]** after payment is verified. Custom or altered pieces may take longer; we will agree a timeline with you before production. Estimated transit times and available destinations are **[insert confirmed details]**. We will share **[tracking method]** when the order is dispatched. If a stated delivery date cannot be met, contact us so we can investigate and arrange a suitable remedy.

**Changes and cancellations.** If you have not sent a UPI payment, you can request cancellation from the private order link. The order and its reserved stock remain open until our team checks the receiving account and confirms cancellation. If you already sent money, submit the transaction reference through the same link or contact us with the order number. Once a payment reference is under review, our team checks the receiving account before cancelling or changing the order. Changes after production or dispatch depend on the item's status and the agreed customisation.

**Returns and exchanges.** Please contact **[support contact]** within **[approved request window]** of delivery, quoting your order number and the reason for your request. Eligibility for ready-made and custom pieces, item condition, packaging, pickup method, and return shipping cost are **[owner-approved rules]**. We will not treat this section as limiting any remedy required for defective, deficient, incorrectly supplied, or otherwise nonconforming goods under applicable law.

**Refunds.** We investigate a refund request using the order details and, for UPI payments, the receiving account statement. If accepted, we will tell you the amount, method, and expected processing time **[approved timeline and method]**. A transaction screenshot or customer-submitted reference alone is not proof that funds arrived. If you sent money but did not submit a reference, contact us promptly so we can trace the transaction.

**Help.** Use the [contact page](/contact) or **[support email/phone]**. Consumer complaints may be addressed to **[grievance officer and contact]**; the team should acknowledge them within the applicable time and provide a resolution or status update.

## Store terms — proposed public copy

**Seller.** This storefront is operated by **[legal seller name and address]** under the JANADHISH brand. Customer support: **[contact details]**.

**Products and prices.** Product descriptions, available sizes, colours, stock, and INR prices are shown on the product page. The order total is calculated by the store at checkout. We may correct an obvious listing error or decline an order that cannot be fulfilled; if we have already received a payment, we will contact you to resolve it and refund any amount due.

**Orders and payment.** Checkout currently offers only the displayed JANADHISH UPI QR. Enter the exact checkout amount in your UPI app and check the recipient before authorising payment. After paying, submit the transaction reference and payer name through your private order link. Submission starts a manual review; the order is marked paid only after our reviewer matches the reference and exact amount against the receiving account. Do not send a second payment because a status update is delayed—contact us first. Keep the private order link secure.

**Custom-made requests.** A measurement or design request is a request for discussion, not a paid order or a final price quote. We confirm specifications, price, and timing separately before taking a custom order. Measurements should be supplied accurately; contact us promptly if you need to correct them.

**Delivery, changes, returns, refunds, and complaints.** The approved [Shipping, returns and refunds](/shipping-policy) policy will set out the applicable process. You can also contact **[support contact]**. These terms do not exclude rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.

**Use of the site.** Provide accurate checkout and contact information, and do not misuse the service or attempt unauthorised access. Product photos and site content belong to JANADHISH or their respective rights holders.
